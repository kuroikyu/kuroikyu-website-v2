## How did you build this website?

Thanks for asking! I mostly didn't, this is a fork of [Sanity's blog starter repository](https://github.com/sanity-io/nextjs-blog-cms-sanity-v3). It had all I needed to get started: Next.js, Sanity (ofc), and TailwindCSS.

## Why a blog?

This is something I've always wanted to do but never allocated enough time to complete the project. Plus. my memory is rubbish so this will be a fun off-site backup.

## Who are you anyway?

Oh, I'm just a random nerd that currently does some programming and project management for a living. I play D&D with a group of friends, tinker with mechanical keyboards here and there, and lurk around Reddit, HackerNews, and GitHub. The usual.

## What kind of content can I expect to see here?

Well, pretty much anything I mentioned above plus video games, maybe some music from time to time, and other geeky stuff. I watched the movie [Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2906216/) recently and I might touch on that in an entry so let's say movies and TV are fair game too.

## When? How often?

There's no schedule at the moment other than a handful of posts I have in mind. As soon as I find something interesting to write about and some time to do it!

Thanks for making it so far, see you soon adventurer! ⚔️
